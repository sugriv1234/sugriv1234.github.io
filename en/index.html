About

Read the people first. Then do the deal.

Over seven years I’ve worked in marketing, fundraising and international business development across Taipei, Paris, Dakar and Abidjan. At Médecins Sans Frontières I coordinated a street fundraising operation of around 40 fundraisers; in Paris I ran a benchmarking study across 11 countries; at the Ritz Paris I learned first-hand how luxury service is built.

In 2026 I founded VicTseng Consult to focus on one thing: helping Asian people and companies understand a new market before they invest in it. My field today is West Africa. The method travels to any new market.
