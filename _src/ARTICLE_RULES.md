# Rules for 「讀懂非洲」 column articles (victseng.com)

Audience: Taiwanese / Asian professionals, companies, travellers and collectors curious about Côte d'Ivoire and francophone West Africa.
Language: Traditional Chinese, Taiwan usage (繁體). Use 象牙海岸 and 阿比讓 (never 科特迪瓦/阿比让). Keep French/local names in Latin script with a Chinese explanation.

STRICT
1. Research every article with WebSearch/WebFetch. Prefer official, museum, UNESCO, university, encyclopaedia (Britannica), IMF/World Bank, reputable media. Every specific fact, number, date must come from a page you actually read; include the year for statistics. If you cannot verify it, leave it out. Never invent numbers, prices, fees, quotes, opening hours.
2. NO personal anecdotes, no first person experience ("我在阿比讓…"). Neutral, practical voice; address the reader as 你.
3. Write entirely in your own words. No copied sentences; no quotes longer than a few words.
4. First mention of Côte d'Ivoire: 象牙海岸（西非、講法語、全世界最大的可可產地）.
5. Respectful: no exoticism, no stereotypes about ethnic/religious groups (describe as common/possible, not universal), no politics, no crime/poverty angles, no health/safety or legal/tax advice (describe and point to the official body).
6. Do NOT write about these reserved topics (they belong to a separate media column): time perception / "tomorrow"; cocoa price & value chain; Wave/mobile money; Nouchi slang / "Ivorian French"; middlemen/agents; coupé-décalé; misconceptions about Africa; Abidjan as "Little Paris"/its neighbourhoods; CFA franc peg; family networks/relationships at work; WhatsApp marketing; countries competing for West Africa / Taiwan's position; Christmas in Abidjan; cost of living; 2027 economic outlook; Taiwan brands in West Africa; francophone vs anglophone West Africa business; negotiation/price talk; contemporary art galleries in Abidjan; 10 things before expatriating; AfCFTA; careers in Africa.
7. Length about 800–1,200 Chinese characters. Structure: 2–3 sentence opening on why it matters; 2–4 `##` sections; closing `## 給亞洲讀者的一句話` (or 給亞洲企業／團隊的一句話) with 1–2 sentences; then a line `---` and `*參考：[來源名](url)；[來源名](url)*`. Tables welcome when useful.

FILE: /home/claude/b3/notes/<slug>/zh.md (create the folder). Do not touch other files. Exact format:
